## Common questions about lawn mowing in Canberra.

### How often should I mow my lawn in Canberra?

Most Canberra lawns need weekly or fortnightly mowing through the warmer months (October to March) when growth is strongest. Through autumn and winter, monthly mowing is usually enough. We adjust your schedule to how your lawn is actually growing rather than sticking to a rigid calendar — Canberra's climate swings between extremes, so flexibility matters.

### What height should Canberra lawns be cut to?

It depends on your grass type. Tall fescue and Kentucky bluegrass (common in Canberra Blend) do best at 50-75mm. Couch and kikuyu can be kept shorter at 25-40mm. Buffalo sits in between at 35-50mm. We adjust mowing height seasonally too — a bit higher in summer to protect roots from heat stress, and shorter heading into winter to reduce frost damage.

### Do you use petrol or electric mowers?

All electric, all battery-powered. We run Makita battery mowers, line trimmers and blowers — charged by solar panels at home. No petrol, no exhaust fumes, and significantly quieter than traditional mowers. You can have your lawn done on a Sunday morning without waking the street.

### Do you mow nature strips and verges?

Yes. We mow, edge and blow nature strips and verges as part of a standard visit when you ask. Most Canberra properties have them, and a tidy verge makes the whole frontage look right.
