## Common questions about hedge trimming in Canberra.

### How often should hedges be trimmed in Canberra?

Most Canberra hedges do best with two to three trims per year. Fast growers like photinia and lilly pilly benefit from a trim in early spring, mid-summer, and again in autumn. Slower species like box hedge and pittosporum usually only need two trims — spring and late summer. Regular trimming promotes denser, healthier growth and prevents hedges from becoming woody and patchy.

### What's the best time of year to trim hedges in Canberra?

The best times are early spring (September-October) after the last frosts, and late summer to early autumn (February-March) before growth slows. Avoid heavy trimming in the middle of a Canberra winter — frost can damage fresh cuts. Light tidying is fine year-round, but the main shaping is best done during the growing season when the hedge can recover quickly.

### Can you reduce the height of an overgrown hedge?

In most cases, yes. How much we can take off in one go depends on the species. Photinia and lilly pilly handle hard cuts well and bounce back quickly. Box hedge is more forgiving than people expect. Some species like pittosporum are better reduced gradually over two or three sessions to avoid exposing too much bare wood at once. We'll assess your hedge and give you a plan.

### Do you remove the clippings and green waste?

Yes, always. Every hedge trimming job includes full cleanup — clippings collected from the hedge and surrounding garden beds, paths blown clean, and all green waste removed from the property. You shouldn't have to do anything after we leave.
