## Regular care, proactive management. Your garden sorted.

Garden maintenance is the anti-fuss service. You pick a day — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly — and Kai rolls up and handles it. No scrambling to find someone when the garden's gotten away from you. No guessing what needs doing. It's set and forget.

Every visit includes the full picture: mowing all lawn areas, edging paths and beds, weeding garden beds before weeds set seed, light pruning of shrubs and plants that need tidying, blowing paths clean. And all of it done with battery-powered tools that are quiet enough you can chat while Kai's working, which means your neighbours never know we've been there. No petrol fumes drifting through the windows, no two-stroke whine on a Sunday morning.

The real magic of regular maintenance is what happens over time. Kai gets to know your garden — what grows vigorously in summer, what struggles in frost, which beds are prone to weeds, what plants need watching. That knowledge means he's proactive, not reactive. He spots things before they become problems. A shrub getting leggy gets pruned before you even notice. A bed that's weed-prone gets weeded before it sets seed. Your garden doesn't deteriorate between visits — it actually improves.

Canberra's climate swings between extremes, so we adjust the schedule and what needs doing as seasons change. Fortnightly through spring and summer when everything's growing, stepping back to monthly in winter. Taller mowing heights in summer to protect roots from heat stress. Lighter touches in frost season. It's a garden that actually gets managed, not just chopped back when it goes feral.

Kai will work with you to find the right rhythm for your garden, then adjust as seasons change.

### Can I get a regular fortnightly or monthly schedule?

Yes, absolutely. Regular scheduled visits are the whole point of garden maintenance. We'll lock in a day and time that works for you — fortnightly, monthly, or whatever cadence makes sense for your garden. You get the benefit of Kai learning your garden over time and being proactive rather than reactive about what needs doing.

Kai does the work himself. Life happens. Just let Kai know and you'll reschedule for the next week or whenever suits you. There's no penalty for skipping a visit — if your garden can wait another week or two, that's fine. The schedule is flexible enough to fit your life.
