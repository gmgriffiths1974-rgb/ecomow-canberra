The price

One fixed price per visit, worked out for your site.

No two complexes need the same work, so Kai prices each site from what it actually needs.

  • Every quote shows the price per visit and the yearly total, ex GST and inc GST, so it can go straight to the committee.
  • The price holds for 12 months from the first visit. Any change after that comes to you in writing, with 30 days' notice.
  • Seasonal work in your scope, like autumn leaves or a quarterly prune, is built into the visit price rather than billed on top.
  • Extra work is quoted in writing and starts only once you approve it.
The work

We quote the scope in your work order, in its own words.

Most strata scopes we quote cover some or all of these:

  • Lawns and nature strips: mown to a height that suits the season, and edged along paths, kerbs, driveways and beds.
  • Garden beds: hand weeded and tidied.
  • Hedges and shrubs: trimmed to keep their shape and height, every visit or twice a year, whichever the scope asks for.
  • Leaves and litter: cleared from paths, driveways and beds, including the entrance and mailbox area.
  • Cobwebs: brushed from stairwells and walkways within reach.
  • Damage and safety: anything broken or unsafe reported to the strata manager.
  • Blow-down last: paths and driveways blown clean as the final job, and green waste taken off site.

Not included unless it's quoted: hedge reductions, tree work, dumped or bulky rubbish, and plants, mulch, turf or fertiliser.

The schedule

Visits that follow the seasons, on a day you agree.

Most complexes need visits every fortnight while the grass is growing and once a month through winter. We count the visits from your work order and show the working on the quote, so the yearly total is easy to check.

  • The visit day is agreed with you once the quote is accepted.
  • If rain stops a visit, Kai comes back within three business days.
  • If something is missed, tell us and Kai comes back to fix it within two working days, at no charge.
Quiet gear

Battery tools, so an early visit won't wake residents.

We use battery-electric tools only: no petrol mowers, blowers or trimmers, and every battery is solar charged. They run far quieter than petrol equipment and give off no exhaust fumes.

A petrol blower under a bedroom window at 7am is exactly the kind of thing residents email their strata manager about. Battery gear means the grounds get done without the complaint.

On commercial sites it means no fumes near air intakes or in enclosed courtyards, and work can carry on during business hours.

FAQs

Questions strata managers ask.

Can you quote through our portal?

Yes. Send the request through SMATA or your own portal, or by email, and the quote comes back the way you asked for it, by the due date. Every quote carries your job number.

How is it priced?

Each site is priced from the work it needs, as one fixed price per visit. The quote shows the price per visit and the yearly total, ex GST and inc GST, and the price holds for 12 months from the first visit.

Do you cover Queanbeyan as well as Canberra?

Yes. We quote strata plans in Queanbeyan, Queanbeyan East, Karabar, Crestwood and Jerrabomberra, and units plans in every Canberra district.

What if the site is different from what you quoted?

When the due date doesn't allow a visit first, Kai quotes from a desktop review and inspects the site in person before the first visit. If the work is materially different from the quote, you get a revised price before anything starts.

What happens if it rains, or something is missed?

If rain stops a visit, Kai comes back within three business days. If something is missed, tell us and he comes back to fix it within two working days, at no charge.

How do you deal with weeds?

Garden weeds are removed by hand at every visit. If your scope asks for weed spraying, say so in the work order and Kai will tell you how he'll handle it before you accept the quote.

Can you do one-off work as well, like a hedge reduction or a clean-up?

Yes. Extra work is quoted in writing, with the price set out before anything starts, and it starts only once the strata manager approves it.
